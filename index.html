China-US Tensions: A 2025 Update

Introduction

The relationship between China and the United States remains complex and fraught with tensions in 2025. Key areas of contention include trade imbalances, the status of Taiwan, and technological competition. These tensions are exacerbated by tariff disputes, geopolitical maneuvering, and differing ideological perspectives. The news snippets below provide further context on the current state of affairs.
